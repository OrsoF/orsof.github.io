## Research Profile

Researcher in Reinforcement Learning and Applied Mathematics, working at the intersection of Reinforcement Learning, Dynamic Programming, Markov Decision Processes, state abstraction, and hierarchical methods for scalable decision-making in high-dimensional environments.

Core topics include state abstraction, hierarchical reinforcement learning, approximate dynamic programming, planning, Markov aggregation/disaggregation, scalable MDP solving, policy iteration, value iteration, Q-value iteration, and stochastic optimization.

## Experience

- Oct. 2025 - Mar. 2026: Quantitative Researcher, Qube Research & Technologies, Paris, France. Developed sequential and explainable decision-making methods for systematic equity trading.
- 2022 - 2026: PhD Researcher in Reinforcement Learning, CMAP, Ecole Polytechnique, Palaiseau, France. Developed abstraction-refinement and progressive state-space disaggregation methods for large Markov Decision Processes.
- 2021 - 2025: Research collaboration in Reinforcement Learning and Combinatorial Optimization, Orange Gardens / CMAP. Worked on latency-constrained service placement for edge-computing infrastructures in an industrial 6G research context.
- Mar. 2022: Research Intern in Hierarchical Reinforcement Learning, Telecom SudParis. Explored server energy and performance optimization under uncertainty.
- Mar. 2020: Machine Learning Intern, SNCF. Modelled and forecasted delay propagation in the rail network using boosted decision trees and Bayesian networks.
- Sep. 2019: Research Project in Market Forecasting, Lusis Trading Solutions. Developed predictive models using LSTM, CNN, and NLP methods on financial time series and news headlines.
