Research profile

Researcher in Reinforcement Learning and Applied Mathematics, working at the intersection of Dynamic Programming, Markov Decision Processes, state abstraction, hierarchical methods, and scalable decision-making.

Experience

  • Oct. 2025-Mar. 2026: Quantitative Researcher, Qube Research & Technologies, Paris, France. Developed sequential and explainable decision-making methods for systematic equity trading.
  • 2022-2026: PhD in Applied Mathematics / Reinforcement Learning, CMAP, École polytechnique, Institut Polytechnique de Paris. Thesis: Hierarchical Reinforcement Learning for Large Scale Problems.
  • 2021 - 2025: Research collaboration in Reinforcement Learning and Combinatorial Optimization, Orange Gardens / CMAP. Worked on latency-constrained service placement for edge-computing infrastructures in an industrial 6G research context.
  • Mar. 2022: Research Intern in Hierarchical Reinforcement Learning, Télécom SudParis. Explored server energy and performance optimization under uncertainty.
  • Mar. 2020: Machine Learning Intern, SNCF. Modelled and forecasted delay propagation in the rail network using boosted decision trees and Bayesian networks.
  • Sep. 2019: Research Project in Market Forecasting, Lusis Trading Solutions. Developed predictive models using LSTM, CNN, and NLP methods on financial time series and news headlines.
